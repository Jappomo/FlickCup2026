Rules

  1. Keep the ball. Flick a figure into the ball. You keep possession while your flicked figure touches the ball first and no opposing figure or keeper touches it last.
  2. Losing it. Miss the ball, or let it come to rest off an opponent, and possession switches.
  3. Fouls. Hit an opponent before the ball and they get a free kick from where the ball was.
  4. Three in a row. A figure can be flicked at most three times in a row. It is ringed in red when it has to rest.
  5. Block flicks. After each attacking flick that keeps the ball, the defender may flick one figure to block, or skip. A block must not touch the ball. The defender can also slide the keeper.
  6. Shooting area. Goals only count if the shot is struck past the dashed line in the opponent's half. Own goals always count.
  7. Corners and free kicks. Both sides set up automatically from their corner layouts. Attacking: the taker, 2 in the shooting area, 3 in midfield and 4 in your own half. Defending: 4 in your area, 4 in your half, 1 in their half and 1 on their shooting line. You then get free set-up moves before the kick (two for a corner, one for a free kick), and the defender blocks after each. Take the kick at any time by hitting the ball; once the set-up moves are used, your next flick must hit it.
  8. Restarts. Throw-ins and goal kicks bring up your nearest figure and push opponents back.

Controls

Mouse and touch. Press anywhere and drag back to aim the selected figure, in any direction. While you pull, the mouse is locked so the screen edge never limits your pull (turn this off in Settings). Tap a figure to select it. Let go and the figure travels the arrow, give or take a little. Snap forward through your start point to hit harder; the gold tick on the meter shows a full snap. Drag your keeper during a block. Hold the middle mouse button and drag to rotate the camera (locked while you aim); Reset cam puts it back, and the arrow next to it picks the view it resets to: Vertical, Broadcast or Eagle Eye. To cancel a flick while pulling, right-click, press Esc, or tap with a second finger.
Controller. Right stick: pull back, then flick it forward, Skate style. Left stick or LB/RB: pick a figure. X: Moment. LT/RT: keeper during a block, curl during Finesse. A: skip block. Y: reset camera. Start: pause.
Keyboard. Tab: next figure. M: Moment. Q/E: keeper or curl. Space: skip block. C: reset camera. Esc: pause.
