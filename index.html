    Rules

    1. Keep the ball. Flick a figure into the ball. You keep possession while your flicked figure touches the ball first and no opposing figure or keeper touches it last.
    2. Losing it. Miss the ball, or let it come to rest off an opponent, and possession switches.
    3. Keeper out. On your turn, with the ball in your own box, you can take your keeper off his rod: he plays as a normal figure, but only inside his box. If he touches the ball outside it, he goes back on the rod and the other side gets a free kick. If he just ends up outside, he walks back into the box at the end of the flick. He can go back on the rod from your next turn. Sending him back while the other side has the ball costs you that block.
    4. Zones. The halfway line and the two shooting lines split the pitch into four zones. For each side: A defence, B defensive midfield, C attacking midfield, D shooting area.
    5. Kick-off. Two figures stand at the ball. The kick-off must be a pass: the figure that kicks off cannot play the next flick.
    6. Fouls. Hit any figure, an opponent or a teammate, before the ball and it's a foul. Ball and figure go back, and the other side gets a free kick.
    7. Three in a row. A figure can be flicked at most three times in a row. A pass that reaches a teammate resets the count.
    8. Block flicks. After each attacking flick that keeps the ball, the defender may flick one figure to block, or skip. A block must not touch the ball. The defender can also slide the keeper.
    9. Shooting area. Goals only count if the shot is struck past the dashed line in the opponent's half. Own goals always count.
    10. Corners and free kicks. Both sides set up automatically from their corner layouts. Attacking: the taker, 2 in the shooting area, 3 in midfield and 4 in your own half. Defending: 4 in your area, 4 in your half, 1 in their half and 1 on their shooting line. You then get free set-up moves before the kick (two for a corner, one for a free kick), and the defender blocks after each. Take the kick at any time by hitting the ball; once the set-up moves are used, your next flick must hit it.
    11. Box limit. In your own penalty box you may have at most 1 figure in the small box by the keeper and 3 in the whole box. At the start of each turn, any extra figures (the ones that went in last) are moved just outside. That includes figures knocked in by the other side.
    12. Restarts. Throw-ins and goal kicks bring up your nearest figure and push opponents back.

    Controls

    Mouse and touch. Press anywhere and drag back to aim the selected figure, in any direction. While you pull, the mouse is locked so the screen edge never limits your pull (turn this off in Settings). Tap a figure to select it. Let go and the figure travels the arrow, give or take a little. Snap forward through your start point to hit harder; the gold tick on the meter shows a full snap. Drag your keeper during a block. Hold the middle mouse button and drag to rotate the camera (locked while you aim); Reset cam puts it back, and the arrow next to it picks the view it resets to: Vertical, Broadcast or Eagle Eye. To cancel a flick while pulling, right-click, press Esc, or tap with a second finger.
    Controller. Right stick: pull back, then flick it forward, Skate style. Left stick or LB/RB: pick a figure. X: Moment. LT/RT: keeper during a block, curl during Finesse. A: skip block. D-pad up: keeper out or back. Y: reset camera. Start: pause.
    Keyboard. Tab: next figure. M: Moment. Q/E: keeper or curl. Space: skip block. G: keeper out or back. C: reset camera. Hold F: frame the ball. Esc: pause.
